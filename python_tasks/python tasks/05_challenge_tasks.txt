CHALLENGE TASKS
Use only concepts covered so far:
Variables, input(), arithmetic operators, type conversion, print()

Challenge 1: GST Bill
Ask for product name and price.
Take GST percentage as input.
Calculate:
GST amount = price * GST / 100
Final price = price + GST amount

Challenge 2: Discount Calculator
Ask for:
- product price
- discount percentage
Calculate discount amount and final price.

Challenge 3: Seconds Converter
Ask the user for number of minutes.
Convert minutes into seconds.

Challenge 4: Days to Hours
Ask number of days.
Calculate total hours.

Challenge 5: Salary Per Day
Ask:
- monthly salary
Assume 30 days.
Calculate salary per day.

Challenge 6: Travel Cost
Ask:
- distance in km
- fuel cost per km
Calculate total travel cost.

Challenge 7: Student Percentage
Take marks in 5 subjects.
Assume each subject is out of 100.
Calculate total and percentage.

Challenge 8: Cloud Storage Cost
Ask:
- storage size in GB
- price per GB
Calculate monthly storage cost.

Challenge 9: Currency Practice
Ask for an amount in rupees.
Ask for a conversion rate.
Calculate the converted value.
(Do not fetch live exchange rates.)

Challenge 10: Bill Split
Ask:
- total restaurant bill
- number of people
Calculate amount per person.
